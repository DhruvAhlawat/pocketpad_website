PocketPad

Turn your phone into a low-latency controller for PC, Mac, Android, or TV — Bluetooth HID with zero host install, plus an optional PocketPad Companion on Windows when you want auto-discovery and the tightest Wi‑Fi path for multiplayer.

  • Bluetooth HID
  • Wi‑Fi companion
  • Gamepad + keyboard/mouse
  • Media & slideshow
  • Layouts & profiles

Why PocketPad

Low-latency, all-in-one control. Stay in one app whether you’re playing with a layout, steering a slideshow, navigating a smart TV UI, or driving WASD plus a desktop pointer — with Bluetooth for instant pairing and Companion-grade networking when speed and multiple phones matter.

What you get

  • Instant Bluetooth connectivity

    Pair the phone over Bluetooth HID—no Companion or drivers on hosts that support the profile. Tap “Prepare for pairing” in the app, then bind from PC, Mac, Android, or TV.

  • Very low latency

    Input paths stay tight on Bluetooth HID and tuned over LAN when you bridge through PocketPad Companion for Wi‑Fi.

  • Works across desktops & living rooms

    The same presets cover keyboard & mouse for PC workloads, multimedia keys and TV-focused remotes, and full Xbox-style layouts for couch gaming.

  • All modes in one app

    Gamepad emulation, WASD clusters, oversized touchpads, media transport keys, and presentation-friendly macros — switch presets instead of grabbing another remote.

  • Full configurability

    Custom layouts with drag-and-edit controls, profile copies, joystick behavior, steering curves, UDP pairings — tune everything to fit your rigs.

  • Built for multiplayer

    Multiple phones can connect together for LAN party sessions — especially crisp over Wi‑Fi with Companion juggling devices on your network.

  • Windows Companion + Wi‑Fi

    Install PocketPad Companion for discovery, KM pairing codes, dashboards, and extremely responsive multi-phone traffic. Bluetooth HID on Windows stays available if you prefer pairing only.

Quick start on Windows

  1. Unpack the ZIP, run pc_companion_ui.exe, and approve SmartScreen/network prompts.
  2. Open PocketPad on your phone (same LAN), tap Connect.
  3. For Universal keyboard/mouse, enter the 6-digit code shown inside Companion Settings.
  4. Prefer Bluetooth? Switch transport in-app, prepare pairing once, bind from Windows Bluetooth settings.
