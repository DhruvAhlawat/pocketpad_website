1. WHAT THE SOFTWARE DOES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PocketPad PC Companion is a Windows host application that:

  • Receives controller, keyboard, and mouse input from PocketPad on your phone over
    your local network (Wi‑Fi / UDP) when you choose that connection mode.
  • Presents a dashboard showing connected devices and live input state.
  • Creates virtual Xbox 360–compatible game controllers in Windows through the
    ViGEm framework when the ViGEmBus driver is installed.
  • May install the third-party ViGEmBus kernel driver during setup if it is not
    already present on your computer, which is needed to emulate Xinput of Xbox gamepads.

The Software is designed to operate on your local network. It is not designed to
collect usage analytics or require a Datronapps account. See the PocketPad Privacy Policy
on the PocketPad website for how information is handled.
