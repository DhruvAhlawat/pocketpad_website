What is PocketPad?

A phone app + Windows PC companion for Wi‑Fi input. Gamepad presets, or Universal for keyboard, mouse, and typing on the desktop.

In one sentence

Your handheld becomes a second screen of controls: fast, configurable, and built for how you play or work on PC.

Gamepad & connection

  • Virtual Xbox-style controls — twin sticks, triggers, bumpers, D‑pad, face buttons, and more (per layout).
  • Many built-in layout presets — from full gamepad to racing, arrow keys, and compact setups; pick what fits the game.
  • Custom layouts & profiles — build your own gamepad, duplicate presets, and save favorites.
  • Tilt to steer (where supported) — map phone motion to stick steering for supported layouts.
  • Auto-discover the PC on the same network, or manual host/port as a fallback.
  • Low-latency input path to the PC companion over your LAN.
Optional wide image

Universal controller (keyboard & mouse)

Remote keyboard and pointer for desktop, browser, or games that need keys + cursor.

  • Keyboard + mouse layout — WASD cluster, on-screen keys, touchpad to move the pointer, mouse buttons, speed tuning.
  • Universal fullscreen + type — large touchpad, send typed text to the PC (Unicode) when the target window is focused.
  • 6-digit pairing on the PC — the companion shows a code; enter it on the phone for a secure key/mouse session.

On the phone

  • Sticks: auto-center, fixed vs floating, double-tap = L3/R3.
  • Connection and optional advanced UDP target.
  • App theme and saved layouts / profiles.
