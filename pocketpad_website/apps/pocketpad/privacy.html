PocketPad Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 29, 2026

PocketPad does not require an account. Controller inputs and layouts stay on your device or local link. Datron does not operate backend servers that store or profile that activity.

Summary

  • No sign-in required; we do not sell personal data.
  • Bluetooth and Wi‑Fi sessions are peer-to-peer on your local link.
  • Optional anonymous usage analytics via Google Firebase when allowed in the app.
  • Free tier may show ads (AdMob). PocketPad Pro removes ads. Purchases go through Google Play.
