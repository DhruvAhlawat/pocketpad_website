PocketPad Overview Details

How PocketPad works

PocketPad splits naturally into two paths: Bluetooth HID sessions that emulate USB-class devices after pairing (no Companion software on the host) and optional Wi‑Fi / UDP Companion on Windows for dashboards, KM pairing tokens, multicast discovery, and extremely low jitter when lots of phones connect at once.

Bluetooth HID

  • No install on hosts that accept standard HID pairings.
  • Separate registration paths for generic gamepad emulation vs composite keyboard/mouse — switch modes requires re-pairing on most OSes following the in-app “Prepare” flow.
  • Media & TV remotes send multimedia scan codes usable with smart TVs, Fire sticks, Kodi, VLC, Netflix UIs.

Wi‑Fi companion (Windows)

  • PocketPad Companion bundles discovery, encryption handshakes for Universal layouts, dashboards, verbose logging.
  • Companion auto-runs LAN listeners optimized for multiplayer — multiple phones negotiating independent sessions concurrently.
  • Microsoft Store rollout: the Datron hub reserves a badge for linking “PocketPad Companion” directly to the storefront once approved.
  • Until then, portable builds live in this site’s downloads/ folder (linked from PocketPad).

Controller surfaces

  • Layouts & full configurability via drag edits, clones, joystick logic, tilt-to-steer, steering curves.
  • Universal fullscreen: huge touchpads, clipboard-friendly typing overlays, WASD slabs, programmable macros.
  • Presentation & media modes map to shortcuts for PowerPoint, Google Slides, YouTube-focused keys, VLC, etc.

Operational tips

  • Wake sleeping hosts — Bluetooth radios pause HID until active.
  • Some TVs mute local speakers while treating the phone like a headset; disable Bluetooth media audio when that happens.
  • Keep Companion + phones on same AP band to avoid jitter; multicast discovery requires same subnet.
